# MOSS: Conference & Product Material Tree

To ensure MOSS is successfully pitched as a star internal product to executives and stakeholders at all levels, we have established a clear, comprehensive "Conference & Product Material Tree."

Below is the complete theoretical structure, highlighting what we currently **have (✅)** and what is **missing (🔲)**.

---

## 1. The "Why" - Vision & Value
**Audience**: Global Executives, Country Managers, Commercial Leaders.
**Goal**: Answer "Why do this? How much money does it save? What is the strategic significance?"

*   ✅ **Project Vision & Executive Summary**
    *   *Current File*: `README.md` (1-page English Summary)
    *   *Current File*: `references/IKEA AI 智能图像工作台 - 商业价值白皮书.md` (Chinese Whitepaper)
*   ✅ **Product Landscape Whitepaper**
    *   *Current File*: `references/MOSS_产品全景与汇报材料.md` (Master document for all materials)
*   🔲 **ROI & Build vs. Buy Model (Missing)**
    *   *Details*: Need to explicitly argue why we are building MOSS internally rather than buying off-the-shelf SaaS (e.g., Adobe Firefly/Midjourney), quantifying the long-term ROI and copyright protection of a custom model.

## 2. The "Show" - Pitch & Demo
**Audience**: Decision-making panels and steering committees.
**Goal**: Answer "How to vividly impress them and secure budget/support?"

*   ✅ **Pitch Deck & Script for Business Executives**
    *   *Current File*: `references/MOSS_PPT汇报大纲与剧本.md`
*   ✅ **Defense Deck for Engineering/Tech Executives**
    *   *Current File*: `presentations/Engineer_Manager_Presentation.md`
*   🔲 **Standard Live Demo Script / Video (To Be Deepened)**
    *   *Details*: A PPT script is not enough. We need a foolproof recorded demo video or a minimalist Figma interactive prototype to showcase the smoothness of Vibe Coding.
*   ✅ **Defense Matrix: Core Q&A and Stakeholder Rebuttals**
    *   *Current File*: `references/跨部门干系人沟通与防守矩阵 (Stakeholder Matrix).md`

## 3. The "What" - Product & Design
**Audience**: PMs, Designers, Frontend/Backend Tech Leads.
**Goal**: Answer "What exactly does MOSS look like, and how does it interact?"

*   ✅ **UI & Information Architecture Sketch**
    *   *Current File*: `assets/moss.drawio` (Three-pane layout sketch)
*   🔲 **Core Product Requirements Document (PRD) (Critically Missing)**
    *   *Details*: R&D needs concrete User Stories (e.g., "As a store co-worker, I want to...") and clear functional boundaries for each module.
*   🔲 **Technical Architecture Blueprint (Missing)**
    *   *Details*: A concrete architecture diagram (Frontend, API Gateway, LLM Inference layer, IKEA SKU calculation layer) to prove technical feasibility to engineering leaders.

## 4. The "How" - Operations & GTM
**Audience**: Internal project team, Seed users (store co-workers, operators).
**Goal**: Answer "What do we do next, and how do we teach people to use it?"

*   ✅ **Product Evolution Roadmap**
    *   *Current File*: `references/产品演进与落地路线图 (Roadmap).md`
*   🔲 **1-Pager Cheatsheet / Onboarding Guide (Missing)**
    *   *Details*: A dead-simple instructional diagram or announcement template for non-technical users when the MVP goes live.
